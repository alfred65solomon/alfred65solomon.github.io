Alfred

Alfred is a private personal assistant built and used by a single person. It runs on a computer in the developer's own home and helps them keep track of their calendar and email.

What it does

  • Reads the developer's Google Calendar to spot schedule conflicts and prepare a daily summary.
  • Reads the developer's Gmail to flag messages that need a reply and summarize the inbox.
  • Sends the summaries to the developer by text message.

What it does not do

  • It cannot create, change, send or delete anything in Google Calendar or Gmail. Access is read-only.
  • It is not available to the public. There are no other users.

See the privacy policy for how Google user data is handled.
